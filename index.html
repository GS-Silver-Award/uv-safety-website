WHY IT MATTERS

The impact of UV damage

UV exposure can affect skin in the short term and over time.

SHORT-TERM

Sunburn & dryness

Immediate inflammation weakens the skin barrier.

COLLAGEN

Premature wrinkles

UV exposure accelerates collagen breakdown.

LONG-TERM

Keratosis & cancer risk

Cumulative damage can create precancerous change.

KNOW YOUR UV

UVA vs UVB: what’s the difference?

A

UVA

Ultraviolet A · long-wave rays

DEEPER
  • Longer wavelength
  • Penetrates into the dermis
  • Drives photoaging
B

UVB

Ultraviolet B · medium-wave rays

SURFACE
  • Shorter wavelength
  • Affects outer skin
  • Primary cause of sunburn

HOW SUNSCREEN WORKS

Two routes. One essential outcome.

MINERAL

Reflects UV

Zinc oxide + titanium dioxide form a physical barrier that reflects UV rays.

→
CHEMICAL

Absorbs UV

Avobenzone, oxybenzone and others absorb UV energy before it harms skin.

BOTH HELP REDUCE SKIN CANCER RISK + PREMATURE AGING

EVERY SKIN TONE

Everyone needs sunscreen.

Melanin offers some natural protection — not complete protection.

Darker skin can still develop sun damage and skin cancer.

Use sunscreen during prolonged exposure.
